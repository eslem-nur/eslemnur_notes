About Me

السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته I am Eslem Nur. I strive to connect knowledge with my heart and code with my mind. I aim to deepen the theological knowledge I acquired during my education at Imam Hatip High School at the Faculty of Theology, God willing, and find ways to share it in today's digital world.

As both a Theology Student and a Self-Developing Developer, I try to create meaningful digital works with modern web technologies.
